Aimfully Books partners with organizations and NGOs around the world that are dedicated to educating their communities. We help introduce art and a little bit of entrepreneurship through immersive storytelling and bookmaking workshops.

How does it work?

We sponsor our published artists to conduct intensive storytelling and bookmaking workshops at your school. Students will develop their book concepts, create video pitches, and learn authentic marketing skills. The workshop culminates in an exciting contest where winners have the chance to have their books published with Aimfully Books!

It’s more than education—it’s a cultural exchange that enriches everyone involved!

In 2012, our uncle was a backpacker with a passion for world cultures and communities. By 2016, Tio Diego started building micro-libraries during his travels, providing educational books to underserved areas. He discovered that sparking creativity required more than just access to books—it needed guidance and follow-through.

This led to the development of interactive storytelling and art workshops that taught children how to conceptualize, create, and market their own books. As the saying goes, “Give a child a fish, and you feed them for a day. Teach them to fish, and you feed them for a lifetime.”

Today, we bring these transformative workshops to schools worldwide through an exciting exchange: traveling artists seeking meaningful cultural experiences deliver our programs. While these artists—often on gap years—share workshops and run engaging contests with your students, they also learn about your community and culture, sharing these experiences globally through social media storytelling.

Together, we foster creativity while building global connections!

Can your community safely host a traveling artist and help organize creative workshops? If so, we’d love to sponsor a visit.

Our vetted artists need basic accommodations: a safe space, lodging, and transportation. Whether you represent a school, afterschool program, or community center, we’ll help connect and iron out the details, and you’ll coordinate directly with the artist.

Your students will receive expert guidance in storytelling techniques and narrative development, the hero’s journey framework, book creation from concept to completion, and marketing skills that are authentic and exciting at any age over 13. The program culminates in an exciting contest where students pitch their book concepts. Winners will have their book concept published through our platform. We document this creative journey, creating lasting memories for everyone involved.

Can’t host an artist? You can still apply for our workshop materials and facilitate the program yourself.
