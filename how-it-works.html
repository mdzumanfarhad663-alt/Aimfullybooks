Questions, answered

Good to know
before you back.

When will I receive my book?

Available titles ship normally. In-progress titles ship after they reach their funding goal and production is complete.

What if a project is not funded?

We will notify you. You can receive a refund or choose to move your support to another title.

How can I submit a book idea?

Use the Crowdfund Your Book path and tell us about your artistic, illustrated, or community-focused project.

Can my organization host an artist?

Yes. Communities can apply to host workshops, visits, and cultural exchange opportunities.
