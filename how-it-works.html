Questions, answered

Good to know
before you back.

When will I receive my book?

Available titles ship immediately. In-progress titles ship once they reach their funding goal and premium production is complete.

What if a project is not funded?

You are fully covered by our Backer Guarantee. Each project has a full funding season to reach its goal. If it falls short, we will notify you and issue a full refund, or you can choose to support another title.

How can I submit a book idea?

Head to our Crowdfund Your Book page and pitch your project. We look for unique, illustrated, and exciting book concepts from creators all over the world.

Can my school or organization host an artist?

Grassroots schools, art centers, and communities can apply through our Sponsored Journeys page to self-host free storytelling and bookmaking workshops or host our ambassadors for a cultural exchange.
