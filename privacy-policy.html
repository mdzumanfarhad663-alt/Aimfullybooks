I. General Information#

1.1. Boundless Backer LLC (“the Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is deeply committed to protecting and respecting your data privacy. This Privacy Policy details our operational frameworks regarding the collection, storage, utilization, and protection of personal information across our digital network.

1.2. This Privacy Policy governs all data interactions occurring on or through our main website located at boundlessbacker.com (the “Website”), including our secure e-commerce systems, crowdfunding portals, and creative program application pages.

1.3. As utilized within this document, “personal information” refers to data that specifically identifies you as an individual, alongside metrics directly linked to your personalized activities, transaction footprints, and behavioral interactions on the Website.

1.4. By utilizing the Website, submitting transaction data, or interacting with our crowdfunding campaigns, you acknowledge your full understanding of this Privacy Policy and explicitly consent to the collection, storage, utilization, processing, and disclosure of your personal information in accordance with these terms and the governing laws of the State of Florida, USA. You additionally consent to the secure international transfer of your data for the primary commercial purposes for which it was originally gathered.

1.5. The Company reserves the right to modify or restructure the provisions of this Privacy Policy at any time at its sole discretion. We will alert users to modifications by updating the "Last Updated" timestamp visible at the base of this page. Your continued utilization of the Website following the posting of modifications constitutes your binding consent to our updated privacy and operational data practices.

II. Collection of Personal and Operational Information#

2.1. The Company gathers personal information through both voluntary submissions and automated digital protocols when you interact with the Website. This data collection occurs across multiple functional touchpoints, including checkout operations, user registrations, and specialized program applications.

2.2. Information Voluntarily Provided: Depending on your interaction level with our platform, the categories of personal data we collect include:

  • Backers and Retail Buyers: Full name, billing and physical shipping addresses, email address, telephone contact numbers, and transaction logs.
  • Independent Creators and Program Applicants: Biographical summaries, media files, digital artwork portfolios, website URLs, video pitch files, financial payout coordinates, and physical location mapping metrics submitted via our Crowdfund Your Book application or Independent Agent portals.
  • Traveling Ambassadors and Hosts: Extended identification data, travel schedules, emergency contacts, local hosting capability summaries, and live multimedia content uploads.

2.3. Payment Data Processing: To safeguard checkout safety, all payment transactions are executed through encrypted, third-party tokenization environments handled directly by Stripe or PayPal. The Company never accesses, views, or stores your raw credit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.

2.4. Information Automatically Collected: To optimize site performance and deliver an optimized e-commerce interface, our servers log operational device metadata when you browse the platform, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, regional geographic indicators, and internet service provider (ISP) metrics;
  • Operating system configurations, browser build types, and unique hardware device identifiers;
  • Chronological timestamps, session tracking durations, navigational click-paths, and conversion histories.

2.5. Minor Compliance Protection (COPPA): In strict accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Company does not knowingly collect, retain, or solicit personal information from any individual under the age of 13. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been logged without verified parental authorization, it will be scrubbed from our systems immediately. If you suspect data ingestion regarding a minor has occurred, please alert our security desk immediately via Contact Support.

III. Tracking Technologies, Cookies, and Analytics Metrics#

3.1. The Website utilizes cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, and similar digital tracking scripts (collectively, "Tracking Technologies") to maintain core platform stability, enhance user personalization, and drive our independent marketing infrastructure.

3.2. Operational Utilization Profiles: We deploy both transient session cookies and persistent tracking scripts to achieve the following performance targets:

  • Essential Functionality: Maintaining your active shopping cart items across browser windows and protecting secure user login sessions during author or agent onboarding application reviews.
  • Behavioral Diagnostics: Gathering aggregated, anonymous data regarding how visitors navigate the platform to identify layout bottlenecks and optimize checkout page conversion flows.
  • Targeted Re-Engagement: Deploying pixel tracking assets to deliver tailored promotional notifications and product drops across external digital networks to backers who have expressed direct interest in our curated releases.

3.3. Third-Party Analytic Frameworks: The platform integrates Google Analytics to systematically evaluate consumer behavior and site interaction metrics. This framework gathers data anonymously—including browser types, referral entry paths, and duration histories—and transmits this data directly to Google servers. To completely bypass this telemetry layer, you may voluntarily install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on provided natively by the provider.

3.4. Marketing Pixel Integration: In strict accordance with our automated audience matching protocols, the Website embeds third-party conversion pixels, including tracking triggers from marketing automation suites like Mailchimp. These pixels evaluate checkout completions and product page interests specifically to refine our transactional communication loops and optimize external digital ad placement performance.

3.5. User Control and Preference Overrides: You retain absolute authority to manage Cookie settings by modifying your native browser preferences to isolate, reject, or purge tracking scripts. You explicitly acknowledge that disabling essential cookies may immediately degrade site functionality, block access to your creator dashboards, or completely interrupt the native WooCommerce checkout pipeline.

IV. Operational Utilization of Personal Information#

4.1. The Company processes and utilizes your personal information to maintain a secure, highly optimized crowdfunding and e-commerce environment. Specifically, your data is deployed across the following core commercial channels:

  • Transaction and Order Management: Executing secure checkout operations, processing payments via third-party gateways, verifying project backing allocations, shipping physical Products out of our Florida hub, and managing photo-proof replacement claims for damaged items under our strict No-Returns policy.
  • Specialized Program Audits: Evaluating creative book pitches, manuscript files, video uploads, and artist portfolios submitted to Boundless Backer for crowdfunding and publishing consideration; coordinating destination logistics with traveling Ambassadors or local host families; verifying entry qualifications for The Creative Stampede Academy; and calculating revenue-sharing distributions for our Independent Agents and Brand Partners.
  • Platform Enhancement: Monitoring aggregated, anonymous user metrics to isolate checkout bottlenecks, optimize layout responsiveness, prevent fraud, and secure site infrastructure.
  • Automated Communication Flows: Providing automated transactional receipt sequences, project tracking updates for active funding seasons, and responding directly to your customer support inquiries.

4.2. Marketing and Communication Consents: By voluntarily providing your contact information during checkout, registration, or application sequences, you grant the Company explicit consent to deliver promotional updates, product drop announcements, and newsletters via email, voice calls, or text messaging channels.

4.3. Compliance and Regulatory Protections: All promotional text messaging and automated communication channels are executed in strict accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). Mobile text messaging and data rates may apply depending on your native carrier plan. Consent to receive promotional marketing is entirely voluntary and is never a mandatory condition of completing a purchase, backing a campaign, or interacting with the platform.

4.4. Opt-Out Protocols: You retain absolute authority to revoke marketing communication privileges at any time without penalty. You may instantly opt out of promotional email pipelines by selecting the "Unsubscribe" hyperlink embedded at the base of our marketing templates. To terminate automated communications or request a manual data purge, you may reach out directly to our security desk via Contact Support.

V. Third-Party Data Sharing and Operational Integrations#

5.1. The Company does not sell, rent, lease, or distribute your personal information to third-party brokers or external marketing agencies for independent commercial use.

5.2. To execute our day-to-day business operations and deliver your products, we share specific, limited data categories with trusted operational partners under strict confidentiality guidelines. These integrated third-party systems include:

  • Logistical and Fulfillment Systems: Sharing your name, telephone number, and physical mailing coordinates with our integrated Third-Party Logistics (3PL) warehouse operators and shipping carriers (e.g., USPS) to pack, fulfill, and ship your orders straight out of our Florida hub.
  • Financial Gateways: Tokenizing and routing checkout details securely to Stripe or PayPal to clear credit card operations.
  • Marketing and Audience Engineering Automated Suites: Syncing your contact information, purchase history, and pixel triggers with Mailchimp to execute automated transactional communication sequences, send funding season notifications, and optimize targeted digital advertisement placements.
  • Analytical Platforms: Sending anonymous interaction logs to Google Analytics to assess web performance.

5.3. The Company retains the absolute legal right to disclose your personal information to external entities if explicitly compelled to do so by lawful mandates, including compliance with judicial subpoenas, court orders, or state and federal regulations. We also reserve the right to transmit your data to protect the physical safety, corporate assets, or intellectual property rights of the Company, our independent creators, or our global backers, as well as to investigate suspected transactional fraud.

VI. Information Security Protocols and Inherent Risk#

6.1. The Company deploys industry-standard administrative, electronic, and physical security measures to safeguard the confidentiality of your personal information. All checkout processes and text transmissions on the Website are shielded utilizing Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) and Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption algorithms.

6.2. Internally, access to your data records is strictly siloed. Only authorized personnel, managers, or compliance officers who require access to execute specific logistical, payout, or application review duties are granted entry to your profile metrics.

6.3. You explicitly acknowledge that no method of digital transmission or electronic storage over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure. While we execute absolute best efforts to protect your private records, the Company cannot warrant absolute immunity from data breaches, server hacks, or malicious cyberactivity. By utilizing the Website, you acknowledge and accept these inherent technological risks.

The Company exercises no control over, and assumes zero liability for, the individual data collection practices, tracking pixels, or privacy policies enforced by external web property owners.
