General Information#

1. AimfulMedia LLC (the “Company,” “we,” or “us”) is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy and appreciates your trust that we will do so carefully and sensibly.

2. This Privacy Policy explains when and how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed with respect to our website located at boundlessbacker.com (the “Website”).

3. As used herein, “personal information” refers to information that specifically identifies you as an individual and information about your activities, such as information about your use of the Website, when directly linked to personally identifiable information.

4. By using the Website, you acknowledge and fully understand this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the laws of the State of Florida. You also consent to the international transfers of personal information for the purposes for which it was collected.

5. We reserve the right to change the provisions of this Privacy Policy at any time. We will alert you that changes have been made by indicating on the Privacy Policy the date it was last updated. Your use of the Website following the posting of such changes constitutes your consent to any changes in our privacy practices.

II Collecting the Information#

6. We collect your personal information in several ways when you visit our Website, including information you voluntarily provide to us (e.g., when contacting us by email or completing a purchase).

7. The personal information we collect from you may include:

  • – Your name;
  • – Your mailing address (for shipping);
  • – Your email address;
  • – Payment information (processed securely via third-party payment processors such as Stripe or PayPal).

8. To protect the privacy of young children, we comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13 without verified parental consent. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete such information. If you believe we might have information from or about a child under 13, please contact us at hello@boundlessbacker.com.

9. When you visit our Website, we may automatically collect certain information, such as:

  • – Your computer’s Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • – Your operating system type and browser type;
  • – The time and date of your visit;
  • – The pages you visit and your purchase history.

10. Some of this information is collected through the use of cookies and similar technologies.

III Cookies#

11. We use cookies to:

  • – Keep track of your movements within the Website;
  • – Help you resume where you left off;
  • – Improve your experience on the Website and deliver tailored content;
  • – Collect analytics data to understand how users interact with our Website;
  • – Deliver advertising relevant to your interests.

12. We use Google Analytics to analyze how users interact with our Website. Google Analytics collects information anonymously, such as your IP address, browser type, and usage patterns. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

13. You can manage cookies by adjusting your browser settings to clear, block, or delete cookies. However, note that disabling cookies may impact your ability to fully use certain features of the Website.

IV Use of Personal Information#

14. We use the personal information we collect to:

  • – Process your orders and requests for goods or information;
  • – Ship products and handle returns or exchanges;
  • – Provide you with customer support;
  • – Send marketing and promotional communications (with your consent);
  • – Improve our Website, services, and overall business operations;
  • – Customize your Website experience;
  • – Comply with legal obligations.

15. By providing your contact information, you consent to receive communications from us, including emails, phone calls, and text messages. You can opt out of these communications at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails or contacting us at hello@boundlessbacker.com.

V Sharing the Information#

16. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy or with your explicit consent.

17. We may share your personal information with trusted third-party service providers, including:

  • – Payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal) to facilitate transactions;
  • – Hosting and data storage providers;
  • – Analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to help us understand Website usage.

18. We may also disclose your personal information:

  • – In response to legal obligations such as regulations, subpoenas, or court orders;
  • – To prevent harm, fraud, or unlawful activity;
  • – To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Company or others.

VI Security of Information#

19. We use reasonable administrative, physical, and electronic measures to protect the confidentiality of your personal information and limit access to it. For example:

  • – We use Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption to protect your data during transmission.
  • – Only employees who need access to your information to perform their duties are authorized to handle it.

20. However, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. By using the Website, you acknowledge and accept this inherent risk.

VII Links to Third-Party Websites#

21. Our Website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Once you leave our Website, your interactions are governed by the privacy policies of the linked sites.

VIII User Rights and Opt-Out Options#

22. You have the right to:

  • – Access, correct, or delete your personal information;
  • – Opt out of marketing communications by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails;
  • – Opt out of analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

23. To exercise your rights, contact us at hello@boundlessbacker.com.

IX International Transfers#

24. If you are located outside the United States, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. By using our Website, you consent to this transfer. Data stored in the U.S. is subject to lawful requests by courts or law enforcement.

X Protection for Children#

25. We comply with COPPA and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Please contact us if you believe we have collected such data.

XI Changes to this Privacy Policy#

26. We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting, and the “Last Updated” date will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the Website constitutes your acceptance of these changes.

27. When you make a purchase, your contact information is shared with Mailchimp for transactional communications and ad targeting.

XII Contact Us#

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email.
