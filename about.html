Why we started

To make friends around the world through creativity.

Educator and favorite uncle Tio Diego created Boundless Backer to connect artists across borders and empower a worldwide community of creatives to unleash their imagination.

On this epic, community-backed journey around the globe, we work directly on the ground—traveling to communities in need to teach free storytelling workshops and build creative spaces for the next generation.

We believe quality books in our hands remind us we are alive. Great stories and gorgeous artwork don’t just entertain us—they capture perspectives, spark imagination, and give us a tangible home to preserve our humanity forever. It just feels good.

Let’s do this together.

Boundless Backer is a community-powered publisher bringing raw human imagination into print.

We curate fiercely independent, artistic, and exciting books created by global creators—often in hard-to-reach places with singular, unheralded perspectives. We cross borders to elevate these creators, connect global classrooms, and protect storytelling.

That is why we award entry to elite entrepreneurship masterclasses through The Creative Stampede, fund permanent micro-libraries, and sponsor life-transforming Sponsored Journeys to advance creative freedom worldwide.
