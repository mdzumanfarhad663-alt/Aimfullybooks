Are you an artist or teacher looking for a meaningful travel experience? Whether you’re on a gap year, planning a backpacking trip, or simply want to explore communities while creating social impact, we want to sponsor your journey.

We’re seeking artists with backgrounds in education to make short visits to welcoming communities. We’ll train you in our storytelling and book-making workshops, which you’ll then share with eager children and young adults in these communities.

In exchange for teaching our workshops, you’ll enjoy authentic cultural immersion. Host families provide accommodation, meals, and local guidance. You’ll document your experiences and temporarily manage our social media platforms to share your unique journey with our audience.

This opportunity is especially valuable for artists developing their own books—finish your project while gaining exposure through our platform.

All you need is your creativity, teaching spirit, and passport!
