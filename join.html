Aimfully Books is a crowdfunding book publisher aiming to generate social impact through art.

We publish fiercely independent, unique, artistic, and exciting books that are world-inspired and created by artists worldwide, often in hard-to-reach places with singular perspectives.

We connect communities and elevate artists to share their gifts and stories. That’s why we offer access to premium lightning-fast education, sponsor life-transforming journeys, and help bring programs to advance creative development in underprivileged areas of the world and at home.

How it works

An active title has been funded, and the book is in stock. Your purchase will be processed immediately, and we will ship the book to you within our standard times. You’ll see a tag like this one:

Active

If you see the title is in progress, it means you have a chance to fund it and save! Become a backer! You will see a bar that look like this:

Pre-order50%

Our books are funded by you, our community. Initial production costs are covered when a book is funded, including the artist’s pay. Once a title is fully funded, we contact you to confirm your address and ship your order.

Your purchase is safe with us. If the book does not reach the threshold, we will notify you and refund you, or you can choose to support another title. Visit your order to read the FAQs.
