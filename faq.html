We ship via USPS Media Mail. Once we hand over your package to USPS, we are not liable for any loss or damage. We recommend adding insurance during checkout to safeguard your order, particularly if you reside in a dense or overworked postal area. We do not provide replacements for misplaced packages. Please be aware that insurance does not cover errors in the provided shipping information. Occasionally, packages may experience delays and show a pre-shipment or in-transit status on the tracking page.

If you did not purchase shipping insurance, kindly contact USPS Missing Mail Claim directly.

We promptly process and ship orders of active books or print-on-demand editions within a day or two. We use USPS Media Mail in the US, which takes 2-10 days. International orders go through USPS Priority Mail International, with delivery in 15 to 21 days or longer, depending on customs. Tracking codes are provided via email.

Please note that expedited shipping and next-day delivery are no longer available. Contact us to inquire about alternative services or special arrangements for large orders.

Our Books

Our books are crafted with high-quality, eco-friendly materials. The covers are durable and embossed, while the pages are sewn for added durability. We take pride in creating art books built to last for years.

Additionally, we offer cost-effective options, including paperbacks and print-on-demand options made within industry standards, to ensure affordability and remain competitive.
