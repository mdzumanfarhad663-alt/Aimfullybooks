For schools and communities

Become a Host.

Could your community welcome a creative ambassador?

Are you associated with a grassroots creative school, art center, or community program in an exciting corner of the globe? If your organization can safely host our ambassador for 7 to 14 days, we would love to sponsor an exclusive, life-changing visit.

Boundless Backer partners with educational centers and NGOs anywhere in the world dedicated to protecting raw human imagination. Our vetted, intrepid ambassadors introduce art and creative entrepreneurship through intensive, immersive storytelling and bookmaking workshops.

Over one to two weeks, our ambassador will guide your students aged 11 and up through narrative structure, the hero’s journey framework, and book creation from concept to completion. The workshop culminates in an exciting contest where students develop book concepts and build video pitches. Winners will have their projects officially published right here on our crowdfunding platform.

In exchange for this elite curriculum, host communities coordinate directly with the ambassador to provide safe accommodations, including lodging, meals, and local transportation. It is a profound cultural exchange that enriches everyone involved. We document this entire creative journey, creating lasting memories.

If your organization cannot host our ambassador, you can still apply to access our masterclass materials and self-host the program.

The story behind the journey

What Inspired This.

In 2012, Diego was a lost backpacker with a deep curiosity for other cultures. By 2016, now a favorite uncle, Tio Diego began building micro-libraries during his travels, delivering educational books to remote communities. He did it quietly, without seeking recognition, simply because it felt good. He knew firsthand that stepping into your own creative vision can be terrifying—especially without guidance and support.

While funding this independently pushed his limits, he quickly realized that sparking imagination required far more than just dropping off books. It needed guidance, architecture, and dedicated follow-through. This realization led to the development of interactive storytelling and bookmaking workshops, teaching aspiring authors how to conceptualize, craft, and market their own books.

Today, our traveling ambassadors bring this curriculum to grassroots schools across the globe. By embedding as guests in welcoming communities, they teach creativity, bridge cultures, and bring raw imagination back to an interconnected human experience.
