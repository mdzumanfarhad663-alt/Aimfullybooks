IV. Shipping, Fulfillment Timelines, and Risk of Loss#

4.1. Fulfillment windows are strictly governed by the funding phase of the selected title at checkout:

  • Available Now (In-Stock) Orders: The Company will process and dispatch available physical inventory from our Florida distribution hub within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation. International orders are routed via USPS Priority Mail International (typical transit range of 15–21 business days).
  • In Progress (Raising Funds) Orders: These campaigns represent projects currently raising capital during their active funding seasons. No immediate fulfillment will occur. Production schedules and shipping dates are tentative, dependent on successful campaign completion, and subject to creator manufacturing timelines. The Company will provide systematic updates regarding these timelines but explicitly disclaims fast delivery guarantees for raw project campaigns.

V. Cancellations, Crowdfunding Variations, and Return Restraints#

5.1. Your rights regarding order modifications are strictly controlled by our automated logistical pipeline and the funding stage of the selected title at checkout:

  • Available Now (In-Stock) Items: Because our store utilizes an automated Third-Party Logistics (3PL) fulfillment system, transactions are instantly routed to our fulfillment center floor for packing the moment they are completed. Consequently, in-stock orders cannot be canceled, altered, or intercepted at any point after checkout, even if the package has not yet been transferred to the carrier. All in-stock physical sales and digital printable downloads are strictly final.
  • In Progress (Raising Funds) Campaigns: You are fully covered by our Backer Guarantee. You may freely cancel your pre-order commitment at any time while the crowdfunding campaign is live and actively raising funds. Once a project reaches its funding deadline and production budgets are locked, all campaign contributions become final and non-cancelable. If a project falls short of its target goal at the end of its funding season, the Company will issue a full refund to your original payment method, or help you seamlessly select an alternative title to back.

5.2. To protect the financial integrity of our independent artists and minimize environmental transit waste, the Company operates under a strict No-Returns policy. We do not accept returns or offer refunds for buyer’s remorse, order errors, or formatting preferences.

5.3. If a physical Product arrives with major manufacturing defects or severe transit damage that compromises the integrity of the piece, the Company will issue a replacement copy at no additional cost to You, subject to availability. To qualify for a replacement, You must contact our support channel within seven (7) days of delivery with photographic proof of the unread Product and its damaged shipping container. You are not required to mail the damaged Product back to us. The Company reserves the sole right to determine if the reported damage constitutes a valid claim for a replacement.

5.4. All approved campaign refunds or project shortfalls will be processed exclusively through the original payment gateway used at checkout (e.g., Stripe or PayPal). The Company cannot issue refunds to alternative bank accounts or credit cards under any circumstances.
