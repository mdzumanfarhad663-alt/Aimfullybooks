Going alone is hard. Making a book—any kind of book, including art books- is often a lonely process. If, on top of that, you need to publish it, market it, and distribute it, forget it; you are in for a long road.

We’ve discovered that with the right education, the help of your friends, and the support of our strong community, we can make it more fun for you.

Skip the publisher chase and Amazon labyrinth—let our community back your book instead. We’ll handle publishing and distribution while you focus on your craft, all while contributing to our social impact mission.

Need help making your book? Take our class to learn professional storytelling and book production—from structure to cover design—and finish your book while you get it funded.

We welcome artists to create unique, gorgeous books for all ages, especially those featuring interesting perspectives. Accepted creators receive access to our storytelling workshops and entrepreneurship program to help them build a career from their art.

Instead of chasing publishers, struggling alone, or navigating the difficult self-publishing route, we believe you’ll find more success by sharing your work with our community for their support. With their backing, we’ll handle the publishing and distribution challenges while you focus on what you love most : your creative work! It’s the best of both worlds.
